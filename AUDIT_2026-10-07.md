# Science Audit — ChromDecode-At — 2026-10-07

Run under `/abai review` and `/abai check` (rubric: `repo-audit/SCIENCE_AUDIT_WORKFLOW.md`; ABAI lesson cards L001–L028).

## Dossier
- **Purpose:** decode *Arabidopsis* spaceflight expression changes into PCSD/AraENCODE chromatin-state enrichment.
- **Headline claims:** 1) flight-repressed genes are enriched in Polycomb chromatin; 2) the repressed class is a
  redox/peroxidase module; 3) SOG1 does not bind it; 4) the bias replicates in ISS roots.
- **Data sources:** NASA OSDR (OSD-37/120/193/217/218/314/406/496/624 + sweep), PCSD, AraENCODE, PlantCADB, JASPAR 2024, GO, SRA SRP136806.
- **Stack:** Python 3.11 (numpy 2.4.6, pandas 2.3.1, scipy 1.15.0, statsmodels), HISAT2/MACS2 on HPC.

## Bottom line
Headline claims 1–3 hold, and claim 4 holds with a better attribution (two ecotypes). Re-running the sweep from public OSDR
counts in the pinned environment reproduced every archived DE count and 12/36-state odds ratio to within 1e-15
(Table S50). The OSD-218/406 v8 calls were also reproduced exactly (S48). The audit found **four sample-coding or
labelling errors** (OSD-314, OSD-218, OSD-406, OSD-624), **one plotting bug** (fig10 stars), **one stale exported table**
and **one wrong accession** (GSE107980). All are corrected in the manuscript, the README and the Pages site. The
largest change is to OSD-314: the "escape from Polycomb" S11 odds fall from 22.4 to about 2.0 once the Mars-gravity
arm is unpooled. The direction holds, the magnitude does not.
